# VDV-Soviet-Airborne
A GHPC mod that converts Soviet motor-rifle infantry into VDV troops by modifying the Obr. 73 everyday field tunic to sport an open collar with sky blue facings, a branch patch on the left sleeve and a *telnyashka* underneath; replacing the helmet with a blue beret; and converting the AK74 into the skeletal folding-stock variant the AKS-74.  

**Installation**  
MelonLoader 0.6.1 is required. Extract the DLL file and /VDV/ into your /mods/ folder so that the three asset bundles are together in /mods/VDV/ and the DLL sits separate.

**Known Issues**  
-Subtle lighting errors created by seams in the mesh which cannot (yet?) be resolved do to the extraction-importation method for a SkinnedMeshRenderer.  
-Dubious historicity of Soviet airborne/airmobile forces operating so close to the front, in direct contact with mechanized ground forces.
